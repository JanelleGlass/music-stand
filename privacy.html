Music Stand privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Music Stand is a personal sheet music reader that runs in your web browser. It has no server of its own, no user accounts, no ads and no analytics.

On your device

The PDFs you add, their names, your markings on them, your favorites and folders, your reading positions and your settings are stored in your browser’s storage on your device. They are not sent to the website.

Camera

If you turn on wink page turning, the front camera is used while a score is open. The video is analysed on your device to notice winks. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Music Stand keeps your scores in a “Music Stand” folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices share the same library. Your markings, favorites and folder names are kept in the same folder, in a file called “Music Stand notes and folders.json”.

  • The app asks Google for access only to files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • It reads your Google account’s name and email address, only to show which account is connected.
  • Your scores, markings and folders travel directly between your device and Google. They are not sent anywhere else.
  • The sign-in Google gives the app is kept on your device and expires after an hour.

Music Stand’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

To stop syncing a device, open Settings › Google Drive › Disconnect. To withdraw the app’s access to your Google account completely, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files in your Drive stay yours: delete the “Music Stand” folder to remove them. To remove everything from a device, delete the app’s website data in your browser settings.
