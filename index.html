Keep the same library on all your devices. Your scores are kept in a “Music Stand” folder in your Google Drive, which also makes Drive a backup of them. Add music with Add PDF: PDFs put into that folder from a computer don’t show up here.

Backup

A backup is one file with all your scores and settings. Export one now and then and save it to Files or iCloud Drive. To copy your library to another device without Google Drive, import the backup there and choose Merge.
